Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 16, 2026

Yieldly ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Yieldly application at yieldly.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

By using Yieldly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address or phone number: Collected when you create an account. Used solely for authentication via one-time passcode (OTP). You choose which one to provide.
  • Credit card selections: The cards you add to your profile, stored to personalize your reward rate results.
  • Points valuation preference: Your chosen valuation mode (Conservative, Default, or Optimistic), stored to persist your settings across sessions.

We do not collect financial account numbers, card numbers, Social Security numbers, billing addresses, or any payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your session.
  • Store and display your saved cards and personalized reward recommendations.
  • Send a one-time passcode to your email or phone when you sign in.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Yieldly uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — provides our database and authentication infrastructure. Your email/phone and card data are stored in Supabase-managed databases. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Twilio — used to deliver SMS one-time passcodes when you choose phone-based authentication. Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio only for this purpose. See Twilio's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your card selections and preferences are retained as long as your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings page within the app. Doing so permanently deletes your card selections and preferences immediately. Your authentication credentials (email/phone) are held by Supabase and will be purged within 30 days of account deletion.

To request manual deletion of your authentication credentials sooner, contact us at ahmedawais672@gmail.com.

5. Data Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level security on our database so that each user can only access their own data. Authentication is handled via time-limited one-time passcodes — we never store passwords. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you — contact us and we will provide it.
  • Delete your account and associated data via Settings or by contacting us.
  • Switch between email and phone authentication at any time by signing in with your preferred method.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ahmedawais672@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Yieldly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are indicated by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
