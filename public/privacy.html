Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 16, 2026

Yieldly ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Yieldly application accessible at yieldly.app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

By using Yieldly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Contact information: Your email address or phone number, used solely for authentication via one-time passcode (OTP).
  • Card selections: The credit cards you add to your profile, used to personalize reward rate results.
  • Usage data: Basic, anonymized data about how you interact with the app (e.g., which categories you search) to improve the service. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • Device information: Standard browser and device metadata (browser type, OS version) collected automatically when you access the app.

We do not collect financial account numbers, credit card numbers, Social Security numbers, or any payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your session securely.
  • Store and display your saved credit cards and personalized reward results.
  • Send authentication codes via email or SMS when you sign in.
  • Improve and maintain the functionality and performance of the app.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Yieldly relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — provides our backend database and authentication infrastructure. Your email/phone and card data are stored in Supabase-managed databases hosted on AWS. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Twilio — used to deliver SMS one-time passcodes when you choose phone authentication. Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio solely for this purpose. See Twilio's Privacy Policy.

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes beyond delivering the services.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information (email/phone and card selections) is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. Anonymized usage data may be retained indefinitely.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), row-level security on our database, and secure token-based authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of SMS authentication at any time by switching to email-based login.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@yieldly.app.

7. Children's Privacy

Yieldly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
